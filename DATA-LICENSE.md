# Data license and attribution

`docs/data.json` is derived from the **Neurophysiological Dataset of Stress Resilience During Human-Computer Interaction** (version 1.0.0) by Shotabdi Roy and Joseph Nuamah, published on PhysioNet.

- Source: https://physionet.org/content/neuro-stress-resilience-hci/1.0.0/
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.13026/x3vc-p627
- License: Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

## What was changed

For each of the 35 participants, the left and right pupil diameters were averaged, values outside 1–10 mm were removed, and the signal was averaged down from 250 Hz (p01–p24) or 50 Hz (p25–p37, from the separate Tobii files) to 10 samples per second. Event-marker times, sex, age range and CD-RISC resilience score are included. No other signals (EEG, fNIRS, heart rate) are included.
